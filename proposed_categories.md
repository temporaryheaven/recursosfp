# Proposed Additional Categories for Resources Directory

This document outlines additional taxonomy categories proposed for future expansion of the Online Resources Directory for Trainers & Educators.

---

## Area of Application Taxonomy (Proposed)

| Category Code | Area of Application | Description |
| ------------- | ------------------- | ----------- |
| ACC | Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL) | Assistive tech, contrast analyzers, screen reader testing tools, subtitle generators, text-to-speech engines, and dyslexia-friendly tools for inclusive classroom environments. |
| AGR | Agritech, Precision Agriculture & Environmental Management | GIS tools (QGIS), crop monitoring simulators, meteorological databases, soil/irrigation calculators, and forestry management repositories for agricultural VET programs. |
| AUD | Audio Production & Educational Podcasting | Free digital audio workstations (DAW), audio cleanup utilities, sound recorders, and podcast hosting platforms for audio-based microlearning and language training. |
| AUT | Workflow Automation & No-Code Development | Low-code/no-code platforms, API webhook builders, automation engines (n8n, Make), and form automation utilities for streamlining educational administration and student workflows. |
| BAF | Business Administration, Accounting & ERP Sandboxes | Educational ERP simulators, electronic invoicing tools (Facturae), tax calculators, and business accounting sandboxes for administrative and financial VET modules. |
| COM | Team Communication & Video Conferencing | Video conferencing platforms, team messaging channels, real-time virtual classrooms, and remote tutoring hubs. |
| CYB | Cybersecurity & Network Defense Labs | Virtual capture-the-flag (CTF) platforms, packet inspection tools (Wireshark), cyber ranges, vulnerability scanners, and network emulation software for IT security labs. |
| DAT | Data Analysis, Visualization & Business Intelligence | Open-source BI dashboards, data exploration tools, SQL sandboxes, spreadsheet modeling utilities, and chart generators for teaching data literacy. |
| HEA | Healthcare, Nursing & Clinical Simulation | 3D interactive anatomy atlases, virtual patient scenarios, medical terminology databases, and clinical decision simulators for health and nursing vocational specialties. |
| HSE | Occupational Health, Safety & Risk Prevention (PRL) | Occupational risk assessment calculators, ergonomic evaluation tools, PPE matrix databases, and hazard simulation tools for workplace safety training. |
| LAN | Language Learning & Multilingual Translation | Neural translation engines, phonetics and pronunciation trainers, bilingual terminology glossaries, and vocabulary acquisition platforms for bilingual VET. |
| LOG | Logistics, Transport & Supply Chain Management | Warehouse management system (WMS) simulators, freight and customs calculators, multi-modal route planners, and inventory optimization tools for transport/logistics courses. |
| PKM | Knowledge Management & Note-Taking | Interconnected knowledge bases, educator wikis, digital notebooks, and networked note-taking tools for curriculum and study organization. |
| PMT | Project Management & Agile Methodologies | Kanban boards, sprint planners, Gantt chart creators, and workload management tools for student group projects and Scrum/Agile training. |
| VID | Video Creation, Screencasting & Interactive Video | Screen recorders, lightweight video editors, interactive video annotation platforms (e.g., in-video quiz insertion), and subtitle editors for flipped classroom content. |
| VRE | Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality & Immersive XR | WebXR platforms, 3D object viewers, virtual 360° training walkthroughs, and immersive spatial simulation tools for hands-on vocational demonstrations. |

## Thematic Groupings

### 1. Pedagogical & Digital Media Extensions
* **ACC**: Accessibility & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
* **AUD**: Audio Production & Educational Podcasting
* **LAN**: Language Learning & Multilingual Translation
* **VID**: Video Creation, Screencasting & Interactive Video
* **VRE**: Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality & Immersive XR

### 2. Transversal Digital & Professional Skills
* **AUT**: Workflow Automation & No-Code Development
* **CYB**: Cybersecurity & Network Defense Labs
* **DAT**: Data Analysis, Visualization & Business Intelligence
* **PMT**: Project Management & Agile Methodologies

### 3. Sector-Specific VET Branches (Familias Profesionales)
* **AGR**: Agraria / Precision Agriculture & Forestry
* **BAF**: Administración y Gestión / Accounting & Finance
* **HEA**: Sanidad / Health & Clinical Simulation
* **HSE**: Seguridad y Medio Ambiente / Occupational Risk Prevention (PRL)
* **LOG**: Comercio y Marketing / Transporte y Mantenimiento (Logistics & Supply Chain)
